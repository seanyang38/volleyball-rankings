Ranks are within the 35-team 17/18 field. Divisions are reset at ranks 9, 18 and 27 after refitting. The division champion is the Gold round-robin winner, determined by wins and the same pool tiebreaks. Promotion is based on the final rating rather than tournament placement.

Tournament format

D1–D3 have three pools of three. In round two, pool winners enter a Gold round robin, runners-up enter Silver, and third-place teams enter Bronze. Each three-team group plays a full round robin. Every team in D1–D3 plays four matches; there are no elimination brackets or byes.

D4 has two pools of four. After round robin, Pool A first plays Pool B first for 1st–2nd place, second plays second for 3rd–4th, third plays third for 5th–6th, and fourth plays fourth for 7th–8th. There is no D4 elimination bracket. Every D4 team plays four matches.

Pool standings use match wins, set differential, point differential and then original rank. Best of three sets: 25 points, deciding set 15, win by two. Match records are shown as wins–losses.

New scores are added to the original 187 matches, then the current model is refitted. No cross-division matches or automatic promotion rules are added.
